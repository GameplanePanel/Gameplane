# Dashboard styling matches the design again

Selected tabs show their pill again. Several other parts of the dashboard drifted from the design and are fixed: the Settings side menu, table headers, form fields and selects, warning banners, the login SSO buttons, the Servers table and stat cards, and checkboxes. On the Console tab, the recent pod output now sits under the terminal and folds away from its header. [PR #1033](https://github.com/GameplanePanel/Gameplane/pull/1033)
