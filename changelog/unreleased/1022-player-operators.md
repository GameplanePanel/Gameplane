# Show Minecraft operators in Players

The Players tab shows the count and identities of configured Minecraft Java operators, including offline operators, and refreshes them with the player information. The read-only list comes from the server's ops.json file, works independently of live RCON player queries, and distinguishes an empty list from a failed read.
